## Q1. Is there a public cota-área-volumen (elevation-area-volume) table or curve for each of the four reservoirs?

### Takeaway
No full public elevation-area-volume table was found for any of the four. Amaluza has the most material: three academic works use or annex its hypsometric/volume data (Mora 2005 UCuenca MSc annex; Sánchez-Juny et al. 2023 *Ingeniería del Agua*; Larriva 2023 UPC PhD), but their extracts show no numeric rows. For Agoyán, Sopladora and Coca Codo Sinclair, only single-point design figures were found: a level plus a total or useful volume.

### Cited Findings
**Amaluza (Paute-Molino)**
- A Universidad de Cuenca MSc thesis by Diego Esteban Mora (2005, sediment production in the Paute basin) reportedly has an annex table of "accumulated volume values" for the Amaluza, Jadán and Cuenca-Burgay reservoirs. This is the closest thing to a published volume table, but no rows were visible. — [Mora 2005 via ResearchGate (related paper)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/262674494_Actualizacion_del_conocimiento_sobre_la_produccion_de_sedimentos_a_diversas_escalas_en_la_cuenca_hidrografica_del_Rio_Paute); UCuenca repository hit: [rest-dspace.ucuenca.edu.ec bitstream](https://rest-dspace.ucuenca.edu.ec/server/api/core/bitstreams/5bf9f31b-dc3c-4d48-87b0-5efdd59e0d19/content)

Searches for an EPN/ESPOL thesis with an Agoyán bathymetry or volume curve returned nothing specific. — [search context: EPN CD-6571 (other reservoir)](https://bibdigital.epn.edu.ec/bitstream/15000/11864/1/CD-6571.pdf)

### Inferences
- The Larriva 2023 PhD and the *Ingeniería del Agua* paper are the most likely public sources of a numeric Amaluza hypsometric (elevation-area-volume) curve, and may include pre- and post-sedimentation versions. They should be retrieved from an unblocked network.

### Gaps
- No numeric curve rows were obtained for any reservoir.

## Q2. What are the original and current (post-sedimentation) volumes for Amaluza and Agoyán? In which years were bathymetric surveys done?

### Takeaway
Amaluza's design capacity is **120 hm³ total / 100 hm³ useful**, with a maximum normal level of **1991 m a.s.l.** It has lost a large share of capacity: **36.4 hm³** lost by around the late 1990s (Jerves 1999), and about **50 hm³ (~42%)** filled according to a press report from before Mazar was built. It has a long bathymetric record: 34 surveys 1983–1998/2000, cross-sections through 2013, and twice-yearly surveys by CELEC since then. Agoyán is a small pondage of **1.81 hm³ total / 0.76 hm³ useful** (CELEC 2016), flushed every 20–22 days according to a non-official blog. No Agoyán bathymetry was found.

### Cited Findings
**Amaluza: design geometry**
- Total storage **120 hm³**, useful volume **100 hm³** ("capacidad de almacenamiento de ciento veinte millones de metros cúbicos (120 Hm3) con un volumen útil de cien millones de metros cúbicos (100 Hm3)"). — [CELEC Sur, Central Paute Molino](https://www.celec.gob.ec/celecsur/informacion-tecnica/central-hidroelectrica-paute-molino/); English Wikipedia agrees (120×10⁶ / 100×10⁶ m³) — [Wikipedia Paute Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paute_Dam)
- Maximum normal level **1991 m a.s.l.**; minimum normal level **1935 m a.s.l.** — [CELEC Sur Paute Molino](https://www.celec.gob.ec/celecsur/informacion-tecnica/central-hidroelectrica-paute-molino/). Expreso also says Amaluza operates between 1935 and 1991 m a.s.l. — [Expreso (press)](https://www.expreso.ec/economia-y-negocios/nino-amenaza-hidroelectricas-centrales-serian-vulnerables-296330.html)
- Daniel Palacios dam (arch-gravity): height **170 m**, crest at **1994 m a.s.l.**, crest length **420 m**. — [CELEC Sur](https://www.celec.gob.ec/celecsur/informacion-tecnica/central-hidroelectrica-paute-molino/). **Contradiction:** Spanish Wikipedia gives a height of **159 m**, with the same 420 m crest length. — [es.wikipedia Represa de Paute](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Represa_de_Paute)
- **Operating minimum, from press citing CELEC/CENACE:** "el nivel mínimo que debe tener Amaluza para que Paute pueda operar es **1.975** msnm". Other Primicias notes citing CENACE give **1.978 msnm** as the minimum for operation. One note prints the maximum as "1 911", which is a typo for 1991. All of these are press figures. — [Primicias](https://www.primicias.ec/economia/mazar-embalse-paute-riesgo-cortes-luz-mayores-83054/); [Primicias](https://www.primicias.ec/economia/mazar-embalse-hidroelectrica-cortes-luz-84206/)
- **No surface area or reservoir length was found for Amaluza.** Searches turned up only Mazar's figure (31 km maximum length), which belongs to Mazar, not Amaluza. — [Wikipedia Mazar Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mazar_Dam)
- A 1972–1978 construction window is reported, with Phase AB operating in 1983 and Phase C in 1991. The year 1991 refers to commissioning, not to an elevation. — [Wikipedia Paute Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paute_Dam)

**Amaluza: sedimentation and bathymetry**
- "Capacidad de almacenamiento ha ido disminuyendo en **36.4 millones de m³**" over about 15 years, citing **Jerves (1999)**. The same work reports **34 consecutive bathymetries since 1983**, with deposits measured in surveys from 1983 to 2000. — [Mora / Paute sediment paper (ResearchGate)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/262674494_Actualizacion_del_conocimiento_sobre_la_produccion_de_sedimentos_a_diversas_escalas_en_la_cuenca_hidrografica_del_Rio_Paute)
- Another extract counts "34 batimetrías consecutivas desde 1983 hasta **1998**", while a different extract says 1983–2000. The end year varies between extracts. — same source as above
- A December 2013 bathymetry is cited as "CELEC-EP, 2014" in academic literature. The 2023 paper uses 30 cross-sections from 1983–2013. — [Ingeniería del Agua 2023](https://polipapers.upv.es/index.php/IA/article/view/20150)
- CELEC EP Hidropaute "realiza **semestralmente** batimetrías a lo largo del embalse". The annual sediment inflow rate is **2.252 hm³/yr**, and Hidropaute (2005) gives about 2.25 hm³/yr. — [CELEC Sur Paute Molino](https://www.celec.gob.ec/celecsur/informacion-tecnica/central-hidroelectrica-paute-molino/)
- CELEC attributes the silting to high erosion in the Paute basin and to the absence of Mazar dam (which traps sediment upstream) "durante casi dos décadas". — [CELEC Sur](https://www.celec.gob.ec/celecsur/informacion-tecnica/central-hidroelectrica-paute-molino/)
- **Press, El Comercio:** "Los sedimentos llenan el **42%** del embalse de la Central Paute". About **50 million m³** were occupied by sediment, and a Hidropaute dredge removed about **900,000 m³/yr**. The article is undated in the extract but from before Mazar (pre-2010). Another study cites dredging of about **500,000 m³/yr**. — [El Comercio (press)](https://www.elcomercio.com/actualidad/sedimentos-llenan-42-del-embalse/). A later search could not re-confirm the 42%/50 hm³ figure in any other source.
- Model results: opening the bottom outlets significantly improves sediment evacuation. — [Ingeniería del Agua 2023](https://polipapers.upv.es/index.php/IA/article/view/20150)
- A CENACE/Contraloría-related press mention says sedimentation affects Paute Molino's headrace tunnel and requires maintenance. The approach to minimum level raises the risk of mud and debris entering the intake. — [Primicias (press)](https://www.primicias.ec/economia/mazar-embalse-paute-riesgo-cortes-luz-mayores-83054/)

**Agoyán**
- CELEC Hidroagoyán Rendición de Cuentas 2016: "El embalse tiene un volumen de almacenamiento de **1.810.000 m3**, de los cuales **760.000** son de volumen útil." — [CELEC Hidroagoyán RdC 2016 PDF](https://www.celec.gob.ec/hidroagoyan/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/RENDICION-DE-CUENTAS-2016.pdf)
- **Contradiction (non-official blog):** total **1,850,000 m³**, useful **760,000 m³**, maximum level **1651 m a.s.l.**, minimum **1645 m a.s.l.**, and the reservoir is flushed ("lavado del embalse") **every 20–22 days** because of high sediment load. — [represaagoyan54.blogspot.com](https://represaagoyan54.blogspot.com/)
- The CELEC 2015 Rendición de Cuentas shows 100,106,000 m³ total / 90,000,000 m³ useful next to the Agoyán text. This almost certainly belongs to another plant (likely Pucará/Pisayambo) and should not be attributed to Agoyán. — [CELEC RdC 2015 PDF](https://www.celec.gob.ec/hidroagoyan/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/RENDICION-DE-CUENTAS-2015.pdf)
- Dam: concrete gravity, gate-controlled, height **43 m**, crest at **1653 m a.s.l.**, crest length **300 m**, maximum reservoir level **1651 m a.s.l.** Pastaza catchment of 8270 km². — [CELEC Hidroagoyán Central Agoyán](https://www.celec.gob.ec/hidroagoyan/informacion-tecnica/central-agoyan/); [CELEC Agoyán (older page)](https://www.celec.gob.ec/hidroagoyan/index.php/13-centrales/agoyan)
- No surface area, reservoir length, bathymetry or post-sedimentation volume was found for Agoyán. Searches on Tungurahua ash and siltation turned up nothing specific. — [El Universo 2010 (press, flow only: 120 m³/s needed for rated output)](https://www.eluniverso.com/2010/09/26/1/1356/generacion-inestable-tungurahua.html)

### Inferences
- If the 36.4 hm³ loss (Jerves 1999) is taken against the 120 hm³ design total, about 30% was lost by the late 1990s. The El Comercio figure of about 50 hm³ (42%) would put the loss before 2010 at roughly 50/120. The two figures fit a continuing loss of about 2 hm³/yr before Mazar (2010) began trapping sediment.
- CELEC still publishes the design 120/100 hm³. It has not published a current post-sedimentation volume, so its figures are nominal.
- Observed Amaluza levels of 1960–1975 sit well above the design minimum of 1935, which matches an operational floor of 1975–1978 set by sediment near the intake.
- Agoyán's observed band of 1637–1651 extends below the blog's minimum of 1645. Lower readings probably reflect drawdown during flushing (purga).

### Gaps
- No current (post-2013) Amaluza total or useful volume, and no surface area or length at 1991 m.
- No Amaluza dead-storage figure. By subtraction, the design value is 120 − 100 = 20 hm³ below 1935 m, but no source states this directly.
- Agoyán bathymetry, area and current volume were not found.

## Q3. What do the Coca Codo Sinclair compensation reservoir and Sopladora's chamber hold, and what are their areas and min/max levels?

### Takeaway
The Coca Codo Sinclair compensation reservoir has design levels of **1216 (min) / 1229.5 (max) m a.s.l.**, with a crest at **1233.5**. Its volume is variously reported as **0.80–0.91 hm³ useful** and **0.81–1.3 hm³ total**. Since about 2023–2025, sediment has built up in it since the plant started in 2016 and is being dredged. Sopladora has no reservoir, only an **interconnection chamber**: maximum maximorum **1318.14**, normal maximum **1315.40**, normal **1314.07**, minimum **1303.05 m a.s.l.**. No volume or area was found for it.

### Cited Findings
**Coca Codo Sinclair: compensation reservoir**
- Minimum water level **1216 m a.s.l.**, maximum **1229.5 m a.s.l.**, crest **1233.5 m a.s.l.**, total reservoir volume **810×10³ m³**. The levels were chosen to stay below the Napo–Hollín formation contact while meeting the required volume. — [ESPOL thesis 5584.pdf](https://www.dspace.espol.edu.ec/bitstream/123456789/3067/1/5584.pdf)
- The Asamblea Nacional audit report gives operating levels "1.29,50 y 1216,0 m s.n.m." (OCR-garbled, read as 1229.50 and 1216.0). It cites a useful volume of **460,000 m³** that "should rise" to about 800,000 m³. The OCR is unclear. — [Asamblea Nacional, Informe Fiscalización CCS](https://www.asambleanacional.gob.ec/sites/default/files/private/asambleanacional/filesasambleanacionalnameuid-591/INFORME%20FINAL%20COCA%20CODO%20SINCLAIR.pdf)
- CELEC technical page: capacity **800,000 m³**. The reservoir fills during the day for use in the evening peak. — [CELEC Coca Codo Sinclair](https://www.celec.gob.ec/cocacodo/informacion-tecnica/central-hidroelectrica-coca-codo-sinclair/)
- CELEC dredging news: useful volume **880,000 m³**. Testing of a temporary dredging system started on 25 September (year unclear, probably 2023), to remove sediment accumulated since operation began in 2016. — [CELEC CCS news](https://www.celec.gob.ec/cocacodo/noticias/celec-ep-inicia-el-retiro-de-sedimentos-acumulados-en-el-embalse-compensador-de-la-central-coca-codo-sinclair/)
- **Press:** El Universo quotes the CELEC manager giving a useful volume of **908,000 m³**. — [El Universo](https://www.eluniverso.com/economia/produccion/central-hidroelectrica-coca-codo-sinclair-mantenimiento-programado-nota/)
- Ministerio de Telecomunicaciones (2016): compensation reservoir of **1,300,000 m³**, of which **900,000 m³** is useful volume for regulation. — [telecomunicaciones.gob.ec](https://www.telecomunicaciones.gob.ec/coca-codo-sinclair-empezara-a-generar-los-primeros-750-mv-de-energia-limpia-y-renovable/)
- Astec feasibility sheet: "Volumen total de embalse: **812.103 m³**", rockfill (CFRD) compensation dam with a maximum height of **53.5 m**. — [Astec](https://astec.com.ec/proyecto_hidroelectrico_coca_codo_sinclair.php)
- **Dam height contradiction:** CELEC gives a concrete-face rockfill dam **58 m** high, while a 2014 report gives **53 m** and Astec gives **53.5 m**. — [CELEC CCS](https://www.celec.gob.ec/cocacodo/informacion-tecnica/central-hidroelectrica-coca-codo-sinclair/); [Astec](https://astec.com.ec/proyecto_hidroelectrico_coca_codo_sinclair.php)
- **Surface area:** none of the sources found give the compensation reservoir's water-surface area.
- Sediment has reduced output, for example to about 660.9 MW average on 8 October 2026 (press). — [Primicias (press)](https://www.primicias.ec/economia/caudal-coca-codo-sinclair-generacion-energia-electricidad-cortes-luz-apagones-133620/); [Expreso (press)](https://www.expreso.ec/economia-y-negocios/coca-codo-sinclair-reduce-generacion-sedimentos-mazar-toma-relevo-298729.html)

**Coca Codo Sinclair: Salado intake and desander**
- Concrete-face rockfill diversion dam **31.8 m** high, spillway **160 m** net width, desander with **8 chambers**, conveyance tunnel **24.83 km** to the compensation reservoir. Located downstream of the Quijos–Salado confluence. Mean flow of the Coca at El Salado is 292 m³/s, and 222 m³/s is diverted. — [UTN museo energías renovables](https://energiasrenovables.utn.edu.ec/museo/coca-codo-sinclair.html); [Sinohydro Ecuador](https://sinohydroecuador.com/asi-funciona-la-central-coca-codo-sinclair/)
- The base of the intake is at about **1275.50 m a.s.l.** (Játiva, regressive-erosion study). — [energia.org.ec, Játiva](https://www.energia.org.ec/docs/coca-codo-erosion-regresiva.pdf)
- The spillway is designed for 9,750 m³/s (as reported). — [CELEC Ficha GPR 2016](https://www.celec.gob.ec/images/lotaip/2016/Comun/k)GPR_Coca_Codo_Sinclair.pdf)
- No headpond volume or level band was found for the Salado intake.

**Sopladora: interconnection chamber**
- Interconnection chamber levels: maximum maximorum **1318.14**, normal maximum **1315.40**, normal **1314.07**, minimum **1303.05 m a.s.l.** A free-flow connection tunnel runs from Molino's tailrace tunnels to the chamber, with control weirs at Molino's outlets. The intake (tunnels plus chamber) is on the left bank of the Paute, and the underground powerhouse is on the right bank. — [CELEC Sur, Central Paute Sopladora](https://www.celec.gob.ec/celecsur/informacion-tecnica/central-hidroelectrica-paute-sopladora/)
- CENACE 2023 visit report: above **1315.5 m a.s.l.**, small spills start through the flap gates ("clapetas") and the discharge tunnel. There is a risk of air entrainment near **1313 m a.s.l.**, and the plant trip level is below **1312 m a.s.l.** — [CENACE LIQVT-2023-131 PDF](https://www.cenace.gob.ec/wp-content/uploads/downloads/2023/11/LIQVT-2023-131-CUR-1080.pdf)
- No chamber volume or surface area was found.

### Inferences
- The figure of about 0.8 hm³ is consistent across design sources (810×10³ ESPOL, 812,103 Astec, 800,000 CELEC). The higher figures (880,000, 908,000, 900,000 useful / 1.3 hm³ total) differ either in definition (total vs useful) or because they include the gross volume up to the crest or spillway.
- The 460,000 m³ figure in the Asamblea report may reflect a reduced as-built or sedimented useful volume. The OCR is too garbled to confirm this.
- Observed CCS levels of up to 1230.5 slightly exceed the design maximum of 1229.5, which suggests surcharge above normal maximum (still below the 1233.5 crest).
- The Sopladora operating band of 1312–1318 matches CELEC/CENACE: 1312 is the trip level and 1318.14 the maximum maximorum. The chamber's storage is negligible, which fits it being a hydraulic connection rather than a reservoir.

### Gaps
- No area figure for the CCS compensation reservoir or the Sopladora chamber.
- No measured sediment volume for the CCS compensation reservoir.
- No data on the Salado intake's pondage volume or normal pool level.

## Q4. Which elevation datums do these figures use?

### Takeaway
Every source states elevations as "m.s.n.m." (m a.s.l.) with no explicit vertical datum. CELEC's level conventions (Amaluza 1991/1935/1994, Agoyán 1651/1653, CCS 1229.5/1216/1233.5, Sopladora 1303–1318) are internally consistent across CELEC, CENACE and academic sources. This suggests a single project datum for each plant, and it is safe to compare them directly with CELEC's published operating levels.

### Cited Findings
- CELEC technical pages express all levels as "m.s.n.m.", for example Amaluza maximum normal 1991 and crest 1994 m.s.n.m. — [CELEC Sur Paute Molino](https://www.celec.gob.ec/celecsur/informacion-tecnica/central-hidroelectrica-paute-molino/); Agoyán crest 1653 m.s.n.m. — [CELEC Agoyán](https://www.celec.gob.ec/hidroagoyan/informacion-tecnica/central-agoyan/); Sopladora chamber 1318.14 msnm — [CELEC Sopladora](https://www.celec.gob.ec/celecsur/informacion-tecnica/central-hidroelectrica-paute-sopladora/)
- The ESPOL thesis and the Asamblea report use the same 1216/1229.5 m s.n.m. values as CELEC for CCS. — [ESPOL](https://www.dspace.espol.edu.ec/bitstream/123456789/3067/1/5584.pdf); [Asamblea](https://www.asambleanacional.gob.ec/sites/default/files/private/asambleanacional/filesasambleanacionalnameuid-591/INFORME%20FINAL%20COCA%20CODO%20SINCLAIR.pdf)
- Press and CENACE operating thresholds (Amaluza 1975/1978, Sopladora 1312/1313/1315.5) are stated in msnm on the same scale as CELEC's design levels. — [Primicias](https://www.primicias.ec/economia/mazar-embalse-paute-riesgo-cortes-luz-mayores-83054/); [CENACE](https://www.cenace.gob.ec/wp-content/uploads/downloads/2023/11/LIQVT-2023-131-CUR-1080.pdf)

### Inferences
- These are most likely local project datums from the INECEL era (Paute and Agoyán, 1970s–80s) and the Sinohydro/Electroconsult design datum (CCS). They are probably tied to Ecuador's IGM national leveling network, but no source confirms this. Any EIA or bathymetry data in a different datum (for example ellipsoidal heights from GNSS) would need checking against the crest elevations listed above.

### Gaps
- No source names the vertical datum (IGM/La Libertad tide gauge or a local benchmark) for any of the four plants.
