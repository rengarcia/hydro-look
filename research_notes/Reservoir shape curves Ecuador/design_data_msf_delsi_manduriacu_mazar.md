## Mazar (Paute river): official total vs useful volume, area at 2153 m, curve points. Does ~206 hm³ useful reconcile?

### Takeaway
The design figures are consistent with each other:
- NAMO (normal maximum level) is **2153 m**, with **410 hm³ total** storage.
- NAMINO (minimum operating level) is **2098 m**, with **101 hm³** below it (dead storage).
- **Useful volume between 2098 and 2153 m is 309 hm³** (initial design).
- During the probable maximum flood the reservoir rises to 2166 m (dam crest).

The 410 hm³ figure is therefore the **total** storage, dead storage included. It is not the useful volume. Our water-balance fit of ~206 hm³ for 2100–2153 m is about **100 hm³ (~33%) below the design useful volume**. No public bathymetric update or official cota-área-volumen table was found that could explain the gap. No surface area at 2153 m was found in any source.

### Inferences
- **Total vs useful.** 410 hm³ is the total at NAMO 2153 m including 101 hm³ dead storage below 2098 m, and 410 − 101 = 309. So the press "410 hm³" should not be compared with a useful-volume fit. The like-for-like design number for 2098–2153 m is **309 hm³**. For 2100–2153 m it would be slightly less, a few hm³ fewer, but no official point at 2100 m was found.
- **Our fit vs design.** Our fit (~206 hm³ for 2100–2153; ~230 hm³ for 2098–2155.8) is ~75–100 hm³ below design. Possible causes:
  - (a) Sediment deposited inside the live-storage zone since impoundment in 2009–2010. Delta deposits in the upper reservoir would reduce useful volume, not only dead volume.
  - (b) Bias in inflow/outflow terms of the water balance: ungauged inflow, spill or bottom-outlet releases, evaporation.
  - (c) The design curve itself.

  No source quantified post-2010 sedimentation of the Mazar live storage.
- **Remote-sensing points.** If the UG paper's "total capacity" is 410 hm³, its percentages imply about **127 hm³ at 2111.45 m** and **~154 hm³ at ~2123 m**. With 101 hm³ at 2098 m, that leaves only ~26 hm³ between 2098 and 2111 m and ~53 hm³ between 2098 and 2123 m. The upper ~30 m (2123–2153) would then hold ~256 hm³. This bottom-light shape fits a long, narrow canyon reservoir. It also suggests the useful-volume shortfall is unlikely to come from the lower band alone. This is arithmetic on a DEM-based paper whose reference total is unconfirmed; use as a weak constraint.
- **Consultant point.** "~380 hm³ at 2151 m" would imply ~15 hm³ per metre near the top, which corresponds to an area of ~15 km². That is high compared with typical Andean canyon reservoirs of 31 km length. Treat this press number with caution.

### Gaps
- No official **cota-área-volumen table or curve** for Mazar was found in any accessible public source: no CELEC ficha, EIA or diseño definitivo. It likely exists in the Hidropaute/CELEC Sur operating manual or the original design studies (Consorcio/INECEL-era), which do not appear to be online.
- **Surface area at 2153 m** (km² or ha) was not found in any source.
- No published **bathymetric resurvey** or sediment-volume update was found for Mazar.
- The full texts of the UCACUE 2021 thesis and the UG 2024 paper could not be opened. They are the most likely open sources for curve points or areas.

## Manduriacu (Guayllabamba river): run-of-river with a small reservoir? Volume and area at maximum level

### Takeaway
Yes. Manduriacu is essentially **run-of-river** with a small pondage. CELEC's technical page gives the operating levels below; the stated volume of "10.30" is an evident unit typo for ~10.3 hm³, and several sources say "10 millones de m³". The observed 460–493.1 m range extends outside the normal operating band; see Inferences.

| Level | Elevation (m) |
|---|---|
| Crest | 495.40 |
| Maximum operating | 492.70 |
| Normal | 492.50 |
| Minimum normal | 489.40 |
| Minimum extraordinary | 479.30 |

Area at the normal level is **70.50 ha**.

### Cited Findings
- CELEC Coca Codo Sinclair technical page for Manduriacu:
  - Dam: **gravity, roller-compacted concrete (RCC)**; **crest at 495.40 m**; **crest length 358.43 m**.
  - Height: **62.4 m from foundation**, **41.40 m above the current riverbed**; riverbed ≈454 m.
  - Reservoir levels: **maximum operating 492.70 m**, **normal 492.50 m**, **minimum normal 489.40 m**, **minimum extraordinary 479.30 m**.
  - **Area at the normal operating level 70.50 ha**; volume listed as "10.30 m³" (unit typo).

  — [CELEC, Central Hidroeléctrica Manduriacu (información técnica)](https://www.celec.gob.ec/cocacodo/informacion-tecnica/central-hidroelectrica-manduriacu/)
- An operations presentation (Prezi) gives slightly different values: maximum 492.8 m and minimum extraordinary 479.4 m. — [Prezi, Central Hidroeléctrica Manduriacu](https://prezi.com/kalbsvgbz7_d/central-hidroelectrica-manduriacu/)
- The Ministry gives the dam as "presa a gravedad de hormigón convencional vibrado y rodillado de 61,4 m de alto" and the reservoir as "10 millones de metros cúbicos". — [Ministerio de Ambiente y Energía, Central Hidroeléctrica Manduriacu](https://www.ambienteyenergia.gob.ec/central-hidroelectrica-manduriacu/); [MAE, Manduriacu](https://www.ambienteyenergia.gob.ec/manduriacu/)

- In October 2024 the plant reduced output and stopped to remove accumulated sediments (press). — [El Oriente, "Los sedimentos serán retirados en Manduriacu"](https://www.eloriente.com/articulo/los-sedimentos-seran-retirados-en-manduriacu/47204)

### Inferences
- The normal operating band 489.4–492.5 m is only ~3.1 m deep, with ~70 ha at the top. That implies daily-regulation pondage of roughly 2 hm³ (rough product of band × area, not a curve). Total storage is ~10 hm³ at full level. This is run-of-river with small pondage.
- Our observed **493.1 m** is slightly above the 492.70 m maximum operating level and below the 495.40 m crest, so it is consistent with a flood surcharge.
- Our observed **460 m** lies far below the 479.30 m minimum extraordinary level and close to the ~454 m riverbed. It most likely reflects a **sediment-flushing drawdown** (e.g. October 2024), not normal operation.

### Gaps
- No cota-área-volumen table or curve was found.
- No reservoir length was found.
- No dead-storage volume was found.
- The exact volume at the 492.5/492.7 m level ("10.30") still needs confirmation from the original CELEC page or design documents.

## Minas San Francisco (Jubones river): volume and area at NAMO and NAMINO

### Takeaway
Total storage is **~14.4 hm³**, with **6.1 hm³ useful**; the rest is dead storage reserved for sediment over a 30-year design life. The upper end of our observed band matches the published levels:

| Item | Elevation (m) | Source type |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum operating level | **792.86** | U. Cuenca thesis |
| Highest water surface (likely flood) | 795 | press |
| Crest | ~795 | trade site |
| River at dam foot | 741 | press |
| Minimum operating level | ≈783 | inferred from press |

The 792.86 m figure matches our 783.33–792.86 m band exactly. The ≈783 m minimum is inferred from a press report.

A peer-reviewed paper (Ochoa-García 2021) **built cota-volumen and cota-área curves for this reservoir** from 2017 LiDAR and a 2019 sonar bathymetry. It is the best lead for a real curve. No area at NAMO was found in any excerpt.

### Inferences
- NAMO ≈ **792.86 m** (thesis) and NAMINO ≈ **783 m** (press-inferred) bracket the **6.1 hm³ useful** volume. That fits our observed 783.33–792.86 m band, and suggests our band is the full design operating range.

- Total storage of 14.4 hm³ at NAMO implies ~8.3 hm³ of dead storage below ~783 m (subtraction of published figures). Annual flushing means the actual post-2019 curve probably differs from the design curve. The 2019 sonar survey in Ochoa-García (2021) is the closest to an "as-operated" curve.

## Delsitanisagua (Zamora river): volume and area at NAMO and NAMINO

### Takeaway
Delsitanisagua has a **very small headpond**: CELEC gives **604,000 m³ (~0.6 hm³)**, and some press says 640,000 m³. The dam is a concrete gravity structure **31–35 m high** (sources conflict) at ~1493 m, a figure from forum or project fiche sources. No NAMO or NAMINO elevations, area, or cota-área-volumen table were found in any accessible source. The plant is effectively run-of-river: 180 MW, 3 Pelton units, high-head diversion via an ~8 km headrace tunnel.

### Cited Findings
- CELEC news: "el embalse tiene una capacidad de **604.000 m³**", filled for turbine technical tests; concrete gravity dam **35 m high and 115 m long**. — [CELEC, "Energía limpia: llenado de embalse Delsitanisagua…"](https://www.celec.gob.ec/hidroagoyan/noticias-noticias/energia-limpia-llenado-de-embalse-delsitanisagua-reducira-480-mil-toneladas-de-dioxido-al-ambiente/)
- CELEC/Ministry project fiche (GPR 2017; Subgerencia de Proyecto Delsitanisagua): a dam figure of 31 m and location ~1493 m appear in search excerpts. The search excerpts attributed figures inconsistently between the GPR PDF and a forum. — [Ministerio, gprdelsitanisagua.pdf](https://www.ambienteyenergia.gob.ec/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/gprdelsitanisagua.pdf); [CELEC LOTAIP GPR PH Delsitanisagua](https://www.celec.gob.ec/images/lotaip/2016/Comun/k)GPR_PH_Delsitanisagua.pdf)
- A forum fiche reads "presa de hormigón a gravedad de **31 m** de alto por **95 m** de longitud a **1493 msnm**" (non-official). — [SkyscraperCity](https://www.skyscrapercity.com/threads/zamora-hidroel%C3%A9ctrica-delsitanisagua-115-mw-e-c.1664811/)
- An ESPOL blog gives **40 m** height "desde la base del embalse hasta la corona" and an 8,040 m headrace tunnel of 4.10 m diameter. — [ESPOL blog, proyectos hidroeléctricos](https://blog.espol.edu.ec/cbfalcon/proyectos-en-el-ecuador/)
- El Comercio and El Productor give "**640 mil m³**" (press; conflicts with CELEC's 604,000). — [El Comercio](https://www.elcomercio.com/actualidad/negocios/hidroelectrica-delsitanisagua-inicio-operacion-experimental/); [El Productor](https://elproductor.com/2018/10/el-proyecto-hidroelectrico-delsitanisagua-inicio-su-operacion-experimental/)
- Annual sediment-flushing campaigns are said to "recuperar la capacidad de almacenamiento del embalse" (e.g. March 2022; September 2026). — [El Oriente](https://www.eloriente.com/articulo/el-desalojo-de-sedimentos-de-delsitanisagua-culminoacute/37319); [Expreso, Sep 2026](https://www.expreso.ec/economia-y-negocios/central-delsitanisagua-entra-mantenimiento-son-trabajos-realizara-celec-294757.html)
- A CELEC Gensur technical page exists, with a section titled "Estudio de impacto ambiental", but its level and volume content did not appear in the excerpts. — [CELEC Gensur, Central Hidroeléctrica Delsitanisagua](https://www.celec.gob.ec/gensur/informacion-tecnica/central-hidroelectrica-delsitanisagua/)

### Inferences
- With ~0.6 hm³ total and a 31–35 m dam whose crest is at ~1493 m, our observed band of **1469–1491 m** spans most of the dam height. The riverbed is roughly 1458–1462 m if the crest is ~1493 m and the height 31–35 m.
- Readings near 1469 m most likely reflect **flushing drawdowns** rather than normal operation. The normal operating band is probably a few metres just below the crest, but no source confirms it.
