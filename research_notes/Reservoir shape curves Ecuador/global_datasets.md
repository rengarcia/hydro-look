## Q1. Catalogue of candidate global datasets: content, method, resolution/accuracy, minimum size, time span, license, access

### Takeaway
Only three open global products give a full **area–elevation/depth–volume curve per reservoir**: ReGeom (Yigzaw et al. 2018, geometric-shape fits), GRDL (Hao et al. 2024, deep-learning bathymetry) and 3D-LAKES (Huang et al. 2025, ICESat-2 + Landsat). GLOBathy provides bathymetry rasters from which curves can be computed. The first two are keyed to **GRanD IDs**, so they cover only reservoirs that are in GRanD (mostly storage > 0.1 km³). All other candidates give single capacity values (GRanD, GDW, GeoDAR), area or storage time series (GRSAD/GRS, GloLakes) or water levels (DAHITI, Hydroweb, SWOT).

### Cited Findings

**A. Dam/reservoir inventories (single capacity, area, dam height; polygons)**
- **GRanD v1.3**: 7,320 records. v1.3 added 458 reservoirs to v1.1 — [USGS catalog](https://water.usgs.gov/catalog/datasets/1cc31a34-415e-4de0-a401-8e03a710498c/). v1.3 mainly added dams built 2000–2016 (+666.5 km³ storage) — [McGill Hydrolab GRanD page](https://wp.geog.mcgill.ca/hydrolab/grand/).
- GRanD inclusion rule: "the main focus was to include all dams associated with reservoirs that have a storage capacity of more than 0.1 cubic kilometers; some smaller dams and reservoirs were added where data were available." Reservoir outlines were delineated from high-resolution satellite imagery — [McGill Hydrolab](https://wp.geog.mcgill.ca/hydrolab/?p=139); [UNEP/GRID metadata (v1.1: 6,862 records)](https://datacore-gn.unepgrid.ch/geonetwork/srv/api/records/52fc26ae-4065-4b9b-9c1e-75b9b2db7043). No source confirmed whether v1.3 kept exactly the same rule (see Gaps).
- GRanD is now distributed via Global Dam Watch. Reference: Lehner et al. 2011, Front. Ecol. Environ. 9:494–502 — [McGill Hydrolab](https://wp.geog.mcgill.ca/hydrolab/grand/).
- **GDW v1.0 (Global Dam Watch, 2024)**: 41,145 barrier locations and 35,295 reservoir polygons (7,420 km³ cumulative storage) — [Lehner et al. 2024, Sci Data 11:1069, doi:10.1038/s41597-024-03752-9](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11461629).
- GDW download: figshare DOI **10.6084/m9.figshare.25988293.v1** (posted 2024-07-25). It has two zips: a Geodatabase and a Shapefile. The Shapefile zip includes a **comma-delimited text file of barrier attributes**, and each zip has a technical documentation PDF — [figshare record](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Global_Dam_Watch_database_version_1_0/25988293). Technical doc mirror: [JRC FTP GDW_TechDoc_v1](https://jeodpp.jrc.ec.europa.eu/ftp/jrc-opendata/GDW/GDW_TechDoc_v1_delta.pdf).
- GDW license is **CC BY 4.0**. It is mirrored in Google Earth Engine (sat-io community catalog) as barrier and reservoir FeatureCollections — [GEE community catalog: GDW](https://gee-community-catalog.org/projects/gdw/).
- **GeoDAR v1.1** (Wang et al. 2022, ESSD 14:1869–1899): 24,783 dam points, harmonized with GRanD v1.3, and 21,515 reservoir polygons from high-resolution water masks. v1.0 had ~22,560 dams geocoded from ICOLD's World Register of Dams (WRD). GeoDAR resolved ~40% of WRD records, which hold >90% of WRD reservoir area, catchment area and storage. **Proprietary WRD attributes (capacity, etc.) are not redistributed**. Zenodo DOI **10.5281/zenodo.6163413**. Companion GeoDAR-TopoCat: Zenodo 7750736 — [ESSD article](https://essd.copernicus.org/articles/14/1869/2022/); [Zenodo 6163413](https://zenodo.org/records/6163413).

**B. Full AEV / storage–area–depth curve products**
- **ReGeom / "Global Reservoir Geometry Database"** (Yigzaw et al. 2018, WRR 54(12):10372–10386, doi:10.1029/2017WR022040). For each reservoir, the storage–area–depth relationship comes from one of **five regular geometric shapes**, chosen iteratively to minimize error in total storage and surface area. It covers **6,824 GRanD reservoirs**. Relative error of total storage is ≤5% for 66% and ≤50% for 99% of GRanD reservoirs. Validation: remote sensing for 40 reservoirs, plus ground data for 34 reservoirs in the US and China. Zenodo record **1322884**, v1.0.0, 28 Jul 2018 — [Zenodo 1322884](https://zenodo.org/records/1322884); [OSTI](https://www.osti.gov/pages/biblio/1498742-new-global-storage-area-depth-data-set-modeling-reservoirs-land-surface-earth-system-models); [PNNL summary](https://www.pnnl.gov/publications/new-global-storage-area-depth-data-set-modeling-reservoirs-land-surface-and-earth).
- **GRDL** (Hao et al. 2024, *Water Resources Research* 60, e2023WR035781, doi:10.1029/2023WR035781): area–storage–depth curves from **deep-learning bathymetry reconstruction** for **7,250 GRanD reservoirs**. Validation covered 54 reservoirs with bathymetry, 118 with altimetry and 68 with LiDAR. Mean absolute depth error on the 54 test reservoirs was **7.87 m**. **Median nRMSE was 20.6% (area–storage) and 22.1% (area–level)**. Zenodo DOI **10.5281/zenodo.8306604** (earlier record 7726763). Profiles are keyed by **GRanD ID**. The creators warn that accuracy may drop for reservoirs **>100 km²** because of image resampling — [WRR article](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2023WR035781); [ADS abstract](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2024WRR....6035781H/abstract); [Zenodo 8306604](https://zenodo.org/records/8306604); [Zenodo 7726763](https://zenodo.org/records/7726763).
  - The task's "Li et al. 2023 GRBD" and "Hao et al. 2024 AEV curves" most likely refer to this GRDL product. I found **no dataset named "GRBD" by Li et al. 2023** (searched; nothing matched).
- GRDL is used downstream. MSEA-Res (Mahto et al. 2025, ESSD 17:2693) builds storage–elevation curves from SRTM, using GRDL bathymetry where needed (Zenodo 14844580) — [ESSD MSEA-Res](https://essd.copernicus.org/articles/17/2693/2025/). A 2026 EGUsphere preprint ("Global Dam Storage", egusphere-2026-2043) extends GRDL curves when GDW capacity exceeds GRDL's. It fits piecewise-linear, cubic-spline and polynomial (degree 1–5) hypsometric models and keeps the lowest-error one — [supplement PDF](https://egusphere.copernicus.org/preprints/2026/egusphere-2026-2043/egusphere-2026-2043-supplement.pdf).
- **3D-LAKES** (Huang et al. 2025, *Sci Data* 12:1625, doi:10.1038/s41597-025-05911-y): area–elevation relationships and 3-D bathymetry for **510,530 lakes and reservoirs** (98.9% of global surface-water storage capacity). **L1** products come from ICESat-2 and Landsat. **L2** products interpolate and extrapolate L1 and "may contain errors". Zenodo record **13107867**. The paper compares results with GRDL — [Zenodo 13107867](https://zenodo.org/records/13107867); [Sci Data](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-025-05911-y).
- **GLOBathy** (Khazaei et al. 2022, *Sci Data* 9:36): bathymetry for 1.4+ million waterbodies aligned with **HydroLAKES**. It combines max-depth estimates with HydroLAKES geometry (a GIS framework, not measured bathymetry). **30 m raster**. figshare doi **10.6084/m9.figshare.13404635.v1**. Also in GEE (`projects/sat-io/open-datasets/GLOBathy/...`). License CC0 1.0 per the catalog — [UCAR data page](https://data.ucar.edu/dataset/b7666ef2-6296-4a89-8aba-d2e22aa2ecd6); [GEE catalog](https://gee-community-catalog.org/projects/globathy/).
- **Global Waterbody Calculator** (arXiv 2605.16323, 2026 preprint): an open-source Python package for depth–area–volume curves. It draws on HydroLAKES, GLOBathy and GLRDAV, and benchmarks GRDL-type fits (depth–volume R² ≈ 0.958) — [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.16323).

**C. Area / storage time series (satellite)**
- **GRSAD** (Gao group, TAMU): monthly area time series for **6,817 GRanD reservoirs** (6,099 km³ integrated capacity), 1984–2015, built on Pekel et al. 2016 JRC GSW. Version 3 (2019, data updated 2020), on TAMU Dataverse — [Gao group research page](https://hgao.engr.tamu.edu/research/); [TDL Dataverse HGao](https://dataverse.tdl.org/dataverse/HGao).
- **GRS (Global Reservoir Storage)**, same group: monthly area, storage and normalized storage for **7,245 global reservoirs** — [Gao group](https://hgao.engr.tamu.edu/research/test-research).
- NASA operational **Global Water Reservoir (GWR)** product (MODIS/VIIRS) — [MODIS Land GWR](https://modis-land.gsfc.nasa.gov/modgwr.html); [VIIRS GWR](https://viirsland.gsfc.nasa.gov/Products/NASA/GWR.html).
- **GloLakes** (Hou et al. 2024, ESSD 16:201–218): relative and absolute storage for **>27,000 lakes**, 1984 to present. Inputs: G-REALM altimetry (T/P, Jason, Sentinel-3/6), ICESat-2 (from 2018), Sentinel-2 BLUEDOT (from 2016), and GSW Landsat (1984–2020). Data DOI **10.25914/K8ZF-6G46** (NCI Australia). Viewer at globalwater.online — [ESSD](https://essd.copernicus.org/articles/16/201/2024/); [NCAR Climate Data Guide](https://climatedataguide.ucar.edu/climate-data/lake-water-storage-glolakes).

**D. Altimetry / water level**
- **DAHITI** (DGFI-TUM): multi-mission altimetry water levels for lakes, reservoirs, rivers and wetlands. **Free after registration** (so not login-free). CC BY 3.0 — [Space4Water](https://www.space4water.org/s4w/web/softwaretoolweb-app/database-hydrological-time-series-inland-waters); [UN-SPIDER](https://un-spider.org/es/node/11611).
- **Hydroweb** (LEGOS/THEIA; now hydroweb.next): lake/reservoir and river virtual stations. Hydroweb.next also distributes the SWOT PLD — [THEIA catalog: PLD Light](https://hydroweb.next.theia-land.fr/catalog/details/SWOT_PRIOR_LAKE_DATABASE_LIGHT).
- **SWOT Prior Lake Database (PLD)**: about 6 million lakes and reservoirs **≥1 ha**, built by harmonizing the UCLA Circa-2015 lake dataset with reservoir databases. The "Light" version comes as nine continental GeoPackages. A "Geometries" version also exists — [THEIA PLD Light](https://hydroweb.next.theia-land.fr/catalog/details/SWOT_PRIOR_LAKE_DATABASE_LIGHT); [THEIA PLD Geometries](https://hydroweb.next.theia-land.fr/catalog/details/SWOT_PRIOR_LAKE_DATABASE_GEOMETRIES); [PLD paper preprint](https://faye.authorea.com/doi/full/10.22541/au.170258987.72387777/v1).
- SWOT accuracy: lake MAE below 0.5 m is reported in early validation. The L2_HR Water Mask Raster v2.0 often misclassifies water pixels, so **SWOT area is noisy**. Storage estimates from levels are limited mainly by AEV-curve accuracy — [Das et al. 2025, Earth & Space Sci.](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2024EA004178); [Zhan et al. 2026, WRR](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2024WR039711). A 2026 study validated SWOT over **small reservoirs** in Iran using legacy photogrammetric DEMs — [J. Hydrol. Reg. Stud. 2026](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2214581826001278).

- For narrow canyon reservoirs well below 10 km² (all eight targets), **every global AEV product is weak**. ReGeom forces a regular shape and is anchored only on GRanD's single capacity/area/depth values. GRDL's median nRMSE of ~20% was for its validation population, not specifically for small canyon reservoirs. GLOBathy and 3D-LAKES L2 extrapolate from shoreline geometry. ICESat-2 L1 needs track crossings over a <1 km wide water body. With 30 m Landsat/SRTM pixels, a ~200–500 m wide canyon reservoir spans only a handful of pixels, so area time series and DEM-derived hypsometry will have large relative errors. This is inferred from the methods, not from a published error figure for Andean reservoirs.
- For Andean reservoirs in particular, SRTM (2000) predates Mazar (filled ~2009–2010), Sopladora, Minas San Francisco, Delsitanisagua, Manduriacu and CCS (2010s). So above-water DEM hypsometry from SRTM can capture the pre-impoundment valley topography for those reservoirs. That makes DEM-based extrapolation methods potentially *better* for them than for older reservoirs. A local AEV built from SRTM/NASADEM/Copernicus with CELEC's capacity as an anchor is likely more accurate than any global product. This is an inference that the report writer may want to confirm with the bathymetry-focused researcher.
- GDW's CSV attribute table (inside the Shapefile zip on figshare) is the quickest login-free way to check presence and capacity for all eight dams. GEE sat-io offers the same in the cloud.

## Q2. Presence of the eight Ecuadorian reservoirs in each dataset

### Takeaway
Presence could **not be verified** for any of the eight dams because no attribute tables could be accessed. Based on inclusion rules and published sizes, **Mazar** (~410 hm³) and **Amaluza/Paute** (120 hm³ total) are the only two likely to be in GRanD and therefore in ReGeom, GRDL and GRSAD/GRS. Mazar is a GRanD v1.3-era dam, and it is *not* in ReGeom if that product was built on v1.1 (unverified). The other six are small run-of-river or regulating ponds (Agoyán, Sopladora, Minas San Francisco, Delsitanisagua, Manduriacu, CCS compensation reservoir). They are at best in GDW/GeoDAR, the SWOT PLD (if ≥1 ha) and HydroLAKES/GLOBathy/3D-LAKES (if mapped as water bodies), and they are unlikely to have any AEV curve in a GRanD-keyed product.
